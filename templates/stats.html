Hourly net settlement from the inverter's grid meter: import and export are offset within each hour; a net import is priced at the import tariff, a net export at the hourly average RCE (x1.23 from 2025-02-01, 0 for a negative hour). Savings = cost of the house load without PV and battery (less the PV-induced extra load) - net import cost + deposit credit. Shift profit is the part of the savings from grid energy stored at one price and used at a higher one (10 % round-trip loss; the house uses stored grid energy before stored PV). Fixed monthly fees are not included. Download daily CSV.
