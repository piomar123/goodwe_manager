Arrow-thickness scale comparison

X: watts (0-8000, covering the "High power" scenario's 7500W). Y: line thickness (1-12px). Dashed vertical at 6000W = FULL_THICKNESS_WATTS, where all three scales are pinned to reach max thickness - only their shape below that point differs.

Same three scales as the chart above, read as watt ranges instead of a curve - the range for each px step, per scale (computed by sampling each scale's own function, not a separate formula, so it can't drift from what the chart/diagram actually draw). A wildly uneven range next to its neighbors is the "ugly quantization" checklist item made literal.

Backup "in use" is two independent signals: (1) off-grid/fault - active regardless of wattage whenever grid_mode/work_mode reports Fault/Off-Grid. (2) On-grid wattage threshold - only while the grid connection is normal, to tell idle CT-crosstalk noise apart from genuine continuous backup load. Pick a mode below.
